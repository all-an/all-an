Best cryptocurrencies

Ranked by privacy, not market cap. Monero is #1 because its privacy is mandatory — every transaction, no opt-out, so the anonymity set is the whole chain. Zcash's cryptography is arguably stronger per transaction, but shielding is opt-in and most transactions are transparent. Highlighted rows are private by default.

About this data. Transaction times and fees are an approximate, hand-curated snapshot — not live figures. Fees swing with network congestion, so "typical" is a judgement call. Prices are filled in manually and shown with the date they refer to; a dash means nobody has filled one in yet. The privacy columns describe protocol design rather than market state, so they age far better than the numbers next to them.
